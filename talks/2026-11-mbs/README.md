# From carbon pricing support to AI in meta-science

Armando Holzknecht, Department of Banking and Finance, University of Innsbruck
Research seminar, MBS School of Business, 2026

The slides as an HTML presentation (`index.html`) and as a PDF (`Holzknecht_MBS_ResearchTalk.pdf`).

## Contents

The talk opens with a look back at the many-designs study and then presents three projects:

- **In the last episode:** Armando Holzknecht, Rene Schwaiger, Juergen Huber, Esther Blanco, Michael Kirchler (2026),
  “Collective evidence on behavioral interventions targeting carbon pricing support: A many-designs approach with
  55 studies”, *Nature Human Behaviour*.
- **Salient carbon dividends are not enough to generate widespread support for carbon pricing.**
  Rene Schwaiger, Julian Benda, Esther Blanco, Armando Holzknecht, Juergen Huber, Michael Kirchler.
  Preregistrations: [Study 1](https://doi.org/10.17605/OSF.IO/ZFVKE), [Study 2](https://doi.org/10.17605/OSF.IO/BN82W);
  OSF project: [osf.io/zv4p5](https://osf.io/zv4p5/).
- **Large Language Models as Synthetic Populations: Quantifying Between-LLM Heterogeneity.**
  Armando Holzknecht, Christian König-Kersting, David Valenta.
  Pre-registration, materials and code: [osf.io/k7fnh](https://osf.io/k7fnh).
- **Large scale evidence on replicability from the European Social Survey.**
  Armando Holzknecht, Magnus Johannesson, Levent Neyse, Felix Holzmeister.
  Pre-analysis plan 1: [doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23187422](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23187422).

## Viewing the presentation

Open `index.html` in a current browser. The presentation is self-contained: it loads no external scripts or fonts.

| Key | Action |
|---|---|
| → or Space | next step |
| ← | previous step |
| Alt + → | next slide, skipping its remaining steps |
| S | speaker view with the notes |
| F | full screen |
| Esc | overview of all slides |

On slides 37 and 40 the cards can also be scrolled with the mouse wheel. The PDF shows the final state of every
slide, without animations and without the speaker notes.

## Use of AI

This presentation was prepared with the help of AI. Claude (Anthropic; Claude Opus 5.5, used through Claude Code)
built the deck under the author's direction: it wrote the HTML, CSS and JavaScript, laid out the slides, and redrew
the figures from the papers' data and published figures (values extracted from the source files, not estimated by
eye). The slide text follows the wording of the underlying manuscripts and pre-analysis plans. The author is
responsible for the content.

**Speaker notes.** The presentation includes speaker notes (press S). The notes are AI-generated: they were written
by Claude from the manuscripts, pre-analysis plans and study materials and may contain errors. Where the notes and the
papers differ, the papers and pre-analysis plans are authoritative.

The last slide of the presentation carries the same disclaimer.
